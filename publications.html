02 Practice

How an entry gets published

Open by default. A result that cannot be checked is not much of a result. Code produced by the lab is published under the VIASystemsLab organisation on GitHub, datasets follow the FAIR principles, and the source of this site is public.

  • Verified before published

    An entry appears in full only once its title, authors, year and DOI have been checked against the DOI itself, not against a memory of it.

  • Linked to its funder

    Every output carries the project that paid for it, so a reader can go from a paper to the grant and back.

  • Machine-readable

    Each verified entry is also published as structured data in the page source, using the same values as the visible text.
