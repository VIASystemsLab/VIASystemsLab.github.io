02 Pledge

A pledge to open science

Every result we publish is peer reviewed, readable without payment, reproducible from open code and FAIR data, and written and checked by people.

  • Peer reviewed, and readable by anyone

    Every article has been through peer review, is open access, and has a stable link in an open digital archive. We prefer venues that let the accepted version be read without payment, and where a venue does not, we deposit the accepted manuscript or preprint so the work stays reachable. A result nobody can reach is a result nobody can check.

  • FAIR data, open code, reproducible results

    Software and datasets are published where the project that produced them keeps them, following the FAIR principles, and each project record links its own repository. A reported result must be reproducible on fixed, inspectable software: we do not report the output of a system whose workings we cannot examine or rerun identically.

  • Written and checked by people

    We use automated tools where they help, and we remain responsible and accountable for every result that carries our names. What those tools may and may not contribute to the work is set out in our statement on AI and scientific research.
